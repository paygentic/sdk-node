The band is selected by the period's total metered usage, and its rate applies to every billed unit. A band matches when the quantity is less than or equal to its `upTo`, and the first matching band wins; the previous band's `upTo` is the exclusive lower bound, and the first band's floor is 0. Exactly one band carries `upTo: null` and it must be the last. Bounds must strictly ascend. The API refuses a ladder whose bounds are out of order rather than sorting it. Because one rate applies to every unit, a total can fall as usage rises: on a ladder of '100' at '5' then null at '3', 100 units bill 500 and 101 units bill 303. |
